# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering an open, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone participating in the ATEN project.

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Our goal is to encourage constructive collaboration while maintaining a professional and welcoming community.

---

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Welcome constructive feedback and different viewpoints.
- Collaborate professionally and communicate clearly.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.
- Support an inclusive and friendly environment.

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or abusive conduct.
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission.
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

---

## Collaboration Principles

When contributing to ATEN, please:

- Keep discussions technical and constructive.
- Respect architectural decisions unless proposing well-supported improvements.
- Submit clear and well-documented contributions.
- Be open to review and feedback.
- Help improve the project for future contributors.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please open a private issue or contact the project maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed respectfully and handled appropriately.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not align with these guidelines.

Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from project participation.
